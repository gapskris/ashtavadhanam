### E. The `*.dxr` Protected Director Movies & Text Preservation
The text of the performance was locked inside Director DXR files. Rather than re-typing or guessing any content, we built a binary chunk extractor (`tools/generate_db.py`) that reads the raw `XMED` text streams:

1. **`eightfold.dxr`**: Sourced all English commentary, translations, scholar questions, and explanations.
2. **`eightfold(s).dxr`**: Sourced all Sanskrit verses and dialogue turns.
3. **`avdhankala.dxr`**: Sourced the 28-paragraph scholarly treatise on the history, origins, and art of Avadhāna Kalā.
4. **`ashtavadhanam.dxr`**: Sourced the 20-paragraph philosophical treatise on Concentration by Sri Aurobindo Society scholars.
5. **`acknowledge.dxr`**: Sourced the full roster of scholars (Avadhānī Dr. R. Ganesh, Pracchakas: Sri Totadrinathan, Dr. Chinmayi, Dr. Narendra, etc.).
6. **`institu.dxr`**: Sourced the institutional overview of Sri Aurobindo Society and Pondicherry University Department of Sanskrit.
7. **`sas.dxr`**: Sourced the Sri Aurobindo Society organizational profile.

> [!NOTE]
> **Zero Text Modification**: Every paragraph, character, and line break in the modern app's database (`content/data.json` and `js/data.js`) originates directly from these DXR files. Only decoding artifacts (such as chunk offsets `0000 118,` and Kruti Dev typewriter font mis-mappings) were cleaned so that the text renders in true Unicode Devanagari.
